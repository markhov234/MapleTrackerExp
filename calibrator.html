Maple draws its numerals as a fixed bitmap font with a dark outline, so a luminance cut separates glyph from background exactly. Raise the threshold until the outline drops away and the digit strokes stay solid. Min gap is how many blank columns split two glyphs.

How to calibrate

  1. Load a screenshot of your client at the resolution you actually play at.
  2. Drag a region tightly around the EXP number. Nudge the geometry fields until the strip below shows only the digits.
  3. Type what the region really says into Teach. One region with all ten digits in it teaches the whole atlas.
  4. Add regions for level and meso. They should parse with no extra teaching — that is the test that matters.
  5. Copy the profile JSON at the bottom and keep it. The Rust capture loop loads it as-is.

Confidence is the pixel agreement of the worst glyph in the region. Margin is the gap between the best and second-best match for that glyph — a high score with a thin margin means two digits look alike and the region will flip under load. Margin is the number to watch.
