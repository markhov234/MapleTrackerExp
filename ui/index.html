About

How the numbers are produced, and what this program does and does not touch.

The curve

The level table is not shipped as data. It is computed from four piecewise formulas and checked against all 99 published values and the cumulative total before release:

  • Levels 1–5 — (floor(L² / 2) + 15) × L
  • Levels 6–50 — q = floor(L² / 3), then (q + 19) × q
  • Levels 51–89 — trunc(previous × 1.0548)
  • Levels 90–99 — trunc(previous × 2.1)

The recursive bands multiply by an integer and divide last, because previous × 1.0548 in binary floating point can land a hair under the true value and truncate to the wrong integer.

Party EXP

80% of the pool is split by level and 20% by damage dealt, then a size bonus is added to your share. The bonus is 10% per extra eligible member, capped at 30%, plus 5% per extra member who actually damaged the monster.

Because the damage term is continuous, party EXP does not arrive in clean multiples of a single value. That is why a tracker cannot count your kills from EXP alone while you are in a party.

What this program touches

  • No game process memory is read.
  • No keystrokes or clicks are ever sent.
  • No network traffic is captured or inspected.
  • No game files are read or modified.

The assistant runs locally as a set of rules over the numbers on screen. It sends nothing anywhere, and every figure it quotes comes from the same deterministic engine as the panels beside it.
